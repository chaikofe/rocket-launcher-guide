# Safety Procedure

Rocket motors are dangerous. Static-fired and constrained motors can fail violently. Follow a strict procedure every time, and check your local laws before igniting anything.

## The arming plug

The most important safety feature. It is a removable connector between the 12V battery and the relays. With it out, the relays have no power and **no signal — accidental, buggy, or malicious — can fire an igniter**. It is the physical guarantee that the pad is dead while you are near it.

Give it a bright streamer so its state is visible from a distance:
- Plug in the receiver (streamer visible at the pad) = system live
- Plug in your hand = system safe

## Pre-launch (at the pad)

1. Key/controller off, arming plug **out** and in your pocket
2. Connect the igniter leads to the receiver terminals
3. Insert the arming plug
4. Walk back to a safe distance (50 m+)

Nobody approaches the pad from this point on.

## Launch (at the controller)

1. Power on the controller
2. Confirm the radio link is good on the display
3. Confirm continuity ("ARMED", not "NO LOOP") on the channels you intend to fire
4. Flip the master arm switch
5. Flip the pad arm switch(es)
6. Count down
7. Press fire

## After launch / misfire

1. Disarm all switches
2. **Wait at least 60 seconds** before approaching (misfired igniters can fire late)
3. Walk to the pad
4. **Remove the arming plug first**, before touching anything else
5. Disconnect the igniter leads

## Non-negotiables

- Never stand in line with a motor's thrust axis
- Never approach the pad with the arming plug inserted
- Keep fire suppression (extinguisher, water, sand) on hand
- Wear eye protection
- Have a clear abort plan and make sure everyone present knows it

## Abort at any time

Disarm the switches. If in doubt, remove power. Then wait, approach, and remove the arming plug before handling the rocket.
